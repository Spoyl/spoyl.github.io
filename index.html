What is it?

It's a latch that replaces the lugs on your kick drum. It connects to any standard kick drum and allows for quick and easy removal of the drum-head, no detuning required. We're working on making a full kit, but in the meantime you can get the latches to retrofit your own drums.

ᚱ

How it works

Swap the lugs on your kick drum with our lug-latches (we recommend just the resonant side) then keep on playing. When you want to pack up, simply unlatch the drum-head and stack your gear inside.

ᚦ

Why it exists

Portable kits sound bad and standard kits don't move. We set out to create a viable third option, invented the lug-latch, then realised that any kick drum can be made portable!
